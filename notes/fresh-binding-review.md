# Static review before fresh model assessment

Development entity identifiers end in indices and old/new bindings both alternate
A/B by that index. Thus fresh names alone do not rule out a suffix-to-plan shortcut.
The novel VIOLET correction still cannot be answered from old targets, but initial
fresh-name gains should not be interpreted as demonstrated binding lookup.

For the already-reserved seed947 fresh assessment, independently permute the eight
fresh entities' balanced A/B bindings with Random(seed+1931), passed explicitly by
--rebind. Familiar/training bindings and all policies, requests, operations and
information remain unchanged. This is a stronger fresh-binding test chosen through
static workload inspection, before any fresh model result. Denominators of changed
obligations are computed from actual targets, not assumed to remain12. Current
bindings remain visible to every method. Development evidence and its source
snapshots remain unchanged; the ongoing order diagnostic keeps the original bank.

The final assessment will test the selected acquisition recipe on these genuine
new bindings. It is not a claim that development and final inputs are identical
in distribution or that an order effect is confirmed without a matched comparison.

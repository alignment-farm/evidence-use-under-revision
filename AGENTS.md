# Independent evidence-use study

Read README.md first. This study owns workload development, methods, protocols,
implementation, resource sizing, evidence and publication. Construct-2 owns
broader theory and synthesis. Develop and execute the commissioned bounded
question autonomously; explain consequential changes. Do not modify the root
or completed sibling studies. Independent questions need not wait on one another.

## Research practice

- Read closest primary methods and relevant implementations before adapting them.
  Record exact paper versions and code revisions; distinguish inspected methods,
  author-reported results, local evidence and discovery leads. Check whether public
  work answers, narrows or redirects the question before selecting new runs.
- Establish useful acquisition, not merely lower loss, finite gradients or verified
  parameter updates. Diagnose plausible acquisition failures through bounded
  comparisons. Preserve failed runs and assess developed claims on fresh material.
- Preserve the README's EU1–EU3 predictions. A neural win is not required. Close
  on explanatory progress, a demonstrated limitation or a concrete resource limit;
  accepting a publication and closing a broad question are distinct decisions.
- Keep current evidence and correction authority explicit and shared. Develop
  competent contextual and executable alternatives where appropriate; disclose
  addressing, structure, teacher information and operations supplied to each.
- Measure complete useful behavior, changed and unchanged obligations, and costs
  of learning, use and maintenance. Separate investigation from deployment costs.
  A hypothetical longer lifetime does not establish repayment.
- Maintain proportional, inspectable evidence and reproduction instructions.
  Publish findings and identifiable revisions; a separate manuscript is optional.
- For arXiv discovery use the query API, cache responses and use a descriptive
  User-Agent. Use one connection and at most one request every three seconds
  across clients under our control; honor Retry-After. Preserve exact versions
  for separately retrieved full texts.

exceeds memory. Public large-cluster training recipes are precedents, not a
mandate to reproduce their resource use. No new hardware purchase is authorized.

resources when warranted. Keep secrets, environments, caches and base-model
weights out of Git; preserve claim-bearing evidence and small learned artifacts
with enough provenance for review.
